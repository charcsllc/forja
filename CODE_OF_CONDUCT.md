# Code of conduct

Creable is a small open source project and we want it to be a pleasant place to contribute.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that breaks these rules, and may block repeat offenders. If something happens that you would rather not raise publicly, contact the maintainers through the repository's security reporting channel.
